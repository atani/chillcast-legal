Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Overview

ChillCast is a chill hours tracker for fruit trees. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data We Collect

Approximate location data: When you grant permission, ChillCast accesses your device location solely to retrieve regional weather estimates. Coordinates are rounded to 0.1° (approximately 11 km) and sent directly to NASA POWER. They are not sent to or stored on any server we operate.

Saved locations: Orchard locations you save are stored locally on your device using UserDefaults. They are not transmitted to any external service.

Purchase information: In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personal information, analytics, usage data, advertising identifiers, or any other tracking data. ChillCast does not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Third-Party Services

NASA POWER: Regional hourly temperature estimates are retrieved from the NASA POWER API. NASA does not endorse ChillCast.

Apple StoreKit: In-app purchases are handled by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling ChillCast removes all stored data.

Children's Privacy

ChillCast is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our support page.
