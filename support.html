ChillCast Support

Getting Started

ChillCast tracks chill hours for fruit trees using historical weather data. On first launch, set your location using the location button or by entering coordinates manually.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are chill hours?

Chill hours measure the amount of cold exposure fruit trees receive during winter dormancy. Most deciduous fruit trees need a minimum number of chill hours to produce fruit properly.

Which chill models are supported?

ChillCast supports three models: 45°F Hours (traditional, counts hours between 0-7.2°C), Utah (weighted by temperature band), and Dynamic (Fishman-Erez, best for mild climates).

Where does the weather data come from?

Weather data comes from NASA POWER. ChillCast uses regional hourly temperature estimates and displays the latest available data date in the app. NASA does not endorse ChillCast.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the Pro upgrade screen and tap "Restore Purchase". Your purchase will be restored through your Apple ID.
